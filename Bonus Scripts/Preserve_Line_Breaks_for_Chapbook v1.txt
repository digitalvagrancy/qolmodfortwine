==============================================================
PRESERVE LINE BREAKS FOR CHAPBOOK
A small style tweak for Twine 2 stories (Chapbook story format)
==============================================================

WORKS WITH
----------
Chapbook only, inside Twine 2.
It will NOT work as-is in Harlowe, SugarCube or Snowman.


WHAT IT DOES
------------
Normally Chapbook ignores single line breaks and runs the lines
together. This instead makes your story show line breaks exactly
as you typed them in the Twine editor.

Note: passages you've already written may look different after
you add it, because lines that used to run together will now
break where you pressed Enter.


WHAT IS THIS, TECHNICALLY?
--------------------------
It's a "style snippet" (CSS). There's no JavaScript and nothing
to download. You paste it into your story's Stylesheet once.


WHAT YOU CAN TWEAK
------------------
Only one thing. In the code, the word "pre-line" can be changed
to "pre-wrap". This also keeps extra spaces you type, such as
several spaces in a row. Most people should leave it as "pre-line".


INSTALLATION
------------
You do this once for every story (project). It does not carry
over to new stories. Each new story needs it pasted in again.

 1. Open your story in Twine 2.

 2. In the top menu, click Story, then click Stylesheet.

 3. Click inside the text box. If there are already styles
    there, leave them alone. Jump to the very bottom (Ctrl+End
    on Windows, Cmd+Down arrow on Mac), then press Enter once
    or twice to leave a blank line.

 4. Copy everything between the START and END lines below, and
    paste it at the bottom.

 5. Close the window. Twine saves automatically.

 6. Test your story. Line breaks should now appear as typed.

Does order matter? No. Underneath any existing styles is fine.
Paste it once only.

If nothing changes, check you used Story > Stylesheet and not
Story > JavaScript.


--------------------------------------------------------------
CODE (paste into Story > Stylesheet)
--------------------------------------------------------------

/* ===== START: Preserve Line Breaks for Chapbook ===== */

#page article p, article p {
  white-space: pre-line; /* change to pre-wrap to also keep extra spaces */
}

/* ===== END: Preserve Line Breaks for Chapbook ===== */
